## The World

Varyndel is an ancient fantasy world entering the earliest stages of industrialization.
Long before the rise of the represented factions, Varyndel was ruled by a civilization whose achievements have never been matched. Their roads crossed continents, fortresses were carved from mountains, magical infrastructure connected distant regions, and imposing citadels stood where many modern capitals now exist. 

That empire fell centuries ago, leaving behind ruins, buried mechanisms, shattered monuments, and mysteries that still shape the world. After the fall of that empire, the history and land of Varyndel was shaped by a variety of regional leaders, but never again any one faction was nearly as big and powerful as the fallen empire. 

But time doesn’t stand still and all over Varyndel technological progress is on the rise, sometimes driven by uncovered ancient technology and sometimes by pure innovation.

Waterwheels power larger workshops. Primitive steam pumps drain deep mines. Early gunpowder weapons appear beside swords and longbows. Guild foundries manufacture standardized armor components, tools, and musket parts. Engineers experiment with pressure engines, mechanical lifts, and cumbersome siege machines.

But these inventions remain rare, expensive, and sometimes unreliable. They have not transformed Varyndel into a fully industrial society. A steam engine is still a remarkable machine, usually found in an important mine, royal arsenal, wealthy city, or experimental workshop. Most labor is performed by hand, animal, wind, or water.

The visual foundation of Varyndel remains natural from the most northern regions all the way to the scorching heat of the desert in the south.

Across Varyndel, three powers are locked in a prolonged and increasingly destructive conflict:

·       The **Ashen Dominion**, a rising human state using organization, gunpowder, regulated magic, and limited industry to expand its influence.

·       The **Orcish Horde**, a shifting confederation of clans, forge-cities, warbands, and migrating peoples struggling for territory, survival, and dominance.

·       The **Velkyn**, an ancient hidden civilization seeking to reclaim the ruins, knowledge, and magical infrastructure of its forgotten empire.

Each faction believes the other two threaten its future. Each possesses only part of the truth about Varyndel’s past.

The Battle Mode is all about grid-based, isometric tactical combat inspired by Tactics Ogre and Final Fantasy Tactics, sharpened with the grim pacing of Battle Brothers. Elevation, flanking, terrain, status effects, and profession combos all matter. And the battlefield itself is drawn from wherever your armies collide: poisonous swamps, high ridges, pine forests, frozen mountain passes, desert ruins. No two battles share the same ground.

## Professions & Builds

Every character can equip a **primary and a secondary profession**, drawing on the ability pools and stat growth of both. Mix them, exploit the synergies, and build units that would be illegal in a more polite game. There are eighteen professions across the three factions — soldiers and shieldbearers, archers and musketmen, pyromancers and cryomancers, assassins, berserkers, paladins, and more.

Every level-up offers perks from tiered pools — Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary — and each character can only carry so many, so upgrades shape the role they grow into. Combine Glass Cannon with Sacrificial Pact and a Regenerating aura, put some legendary equipment on the character and you've built a one-character army that gets stronger the closer it gets to death.

> Ashes & Blood is a sandbox of strategy, designed to be broken by those clever enough to do it.
