## The World

**Varyndel** is a vast, ancient continent at the dawn of a new industrial age. From the frozen mountains of the north to the scorching deserts of the south, its wilderness is dotted with forgotten ruins, abandoned cities, and remnants of a civilization whose achievements have never been matched.

Long before the rise of today's great powers, the Predecessors ruled Varyndel. Their roads crossed entire continents, their fortresses were carved into mountains, and their magical infrastructure connected distant regions. Then, for reasons nobody fully understands, their civilization vanished.

Centuries passed. Nature reclaimed their cities, kingdoms rose and fell, and much of their knowledge was lost. Yet the legacy of the Predecessors remains, buried beneath the soil and hidden among the ruins of Varyndel.

**Now, a new age is beginning.**

Waterwheels drive great workshops, primitive steam engines power mines, and early gunpowder weapons stand alongside swords, bows, and sorcery. But these innovations remain rare and expensive. Most of Varyndel is still a land of untamed wilderness, ancient strongholds, and traditional medieval societies.

At the center of this emerging age lies an increasingly desperate struggle for the artifacts of the Predecessors. These mysterious devices hold tremendous power, promising wealth, technological progress, and military supremacy to whoever controls them. Yet their true nature remains poorly understood, and their power can be as dangerous as it is valuable.

Three great powers have turned their attention toward Varyndel, sending expeditions to explore its uncharted regions, establish settlements, and recover the secrets of the fallen civilization.

But the deeper they venture into Varyndel, the more questions arise.

**What happened to the Predecessors? And what dangers might still lie buried beneath their ruins?**

## Expeditions & Progression

Your journey through Varyndel unfolds across a branching atlas of regions, each offering new environments, challenges, and rewards.

Every expedition takes you into a procedurally generated world with its own objectives. Establish cities, develop your economy, and conquer rival territories in classic 4X-inspired campaigns, or lead a small band of warriors through hostile wilderness in search of ancient ruins. Other expeditions may challenge you to survive overwhelming invasions or fight for control of strategically important locations.

**But your journey doesn't end with a single expedition.**

Bring your surviving veterans home, strengthen them with permanent upgrades, and send them into increasingly challenging regions. Recover valuable resources to unlock new professions, abilities, buildings, and bonuses through a persistent roguelite progression system.

With every successful expedition, your options grow, your armies evolve, and new parts of Varyndel become accessible.

When armies clash, the game shifts into grid-based, isometric tactical combat, inspired by Tactics Ogre and Final Fantasy Tactics, with the unforgiving tactical depth of Battle Brothers.

Elevation, flanking, terrain, status effects, and profession combinations all matter. Position your units carefully, exploit enemy weaknesses, and use the environment to your advantage.

Every battlefield reflects the region where your armies collide. Fight through poisonous swamps, dense forests, frozen mountain passes, scorching deserts, and ruins of the Predecessors.

No two battles need play out the same way.

## Professions & Builds

Every character can combine a **primary and secondary profession**, drawing abilities from both to create powerful combinations. Mix them, exploit synergies, and build units that would be illegal in a more polite game.

Choose from a diverse roster of professions across the three factions, including soldiers, archers, musketmen, pyromancers, cryomancers, assassins, berserkers, paladins, and more.

**But professions are only the beginning.**

As your characters grow, they'll gain access to permanent upgrades ranging from Common to Legendary. Combine powerful perks, specialized abilities, and legendary equipment to create increasingly ridiculous character builds.

Want to combine Glass Cannon with Sacrificial Pact and a Regenerating aura to create a one-character army that gets stronger the closer it gets to death? Go ahead. That's the point.

And with surviving veterans carrying their experience and upgrades into future expeditions, even the most humble recruit can eventually become a terrifying force on the battlefield.

> Ashes & Blood is a playground of strategy, experimentation, and ever-growing power — designed to be broken by those clever enough to do it.

Three playable factions compete for control of Varyndel and the forgotten legacy of the Predecessors. Each brings its own units, professions, abilities, and approach to warfare.
