# Ashes & Blood

<div class="key-art"><img src="/images/key-art.png" alt="Ashes & Blood key art" /></div>

**Ashes & Blood is a fantasy tactics RPG where every battle is brutal, every campaign is different, and the most satisfying victories come from builds the game probably wasn't supposed to allow.**

Lead one of three factions through a war-torn medieval world: the Iron Dominion, a semi-industrialized human empire that conquers to survive; the Orcish Horde, driven into frenzy by something ancient whispering in the dark; or the Velkyn, shadow-born remnants of a civilization erased from history. Each plays — and breaks — differently.

## How It Plays

Every campaign opens with a simple question: how do you want to fight? Crush your enemies in melee, closing the gap with fast, mobile units before they can set their line. Play the ranged game — artillery and massed missile troops that suppress an advance and bury it under a hail of fire. Or lean into magic, sundering the field with arcane spells and summons. The game rarely tells you no.

Battles are fought on ground pulled from wherever your armies collide — poisonous swamps, high ridges, pine forests, frozen mountain passes, desert ruins. Elevation, flanking, terrain, and status effects all bite: strike from higher ground for better odds, land a hit on a flank or back for the kill, and watch Burning, Bleeding, Slowed, and Stunned reshape a fight turn by turn. No two battles share the same ground.

Campaigns are short and dense — two to four hours, no ten-turn construction queues, every action spent from a tight pool of **orders**. The magic is the snowball: a character you've built and rebuilt until they're unlike anyone else on the field, getting exponentially stronger as the run goes on. You'll grow attached — which is exactly why ironman is the default, the AI plays to win, and undoing a turn costs you. Win, and your unlocks echo forward into the next run.

## Two Modes, One Campaign

**World Mode** is a 4X sandbox where you raise cities, recruit armies, and carve territory across procedurally generated maps spanning a multitude of biomes. Each biome shifts resource yields, building options, and faction bonuses — so *where* you expand matters as much as how fast. And forget ten-turn construction queues: actions resolve instantly. The real constraint is orders — a limited pool of commands per turn that forces you to pick your moves carefully, even when your treasury is overflowing.

**Battle Mode** is grid-based, isometric tactical combat inspired by Tactics Ogre and Final Fantasy Tactics, sharpened with the grim pacing of Battle Brothers. Elevation, flanking, terrain, status effects, and profession combos all matter — and the battlefield itself is drawn from wherever your armies collide.

## Professions & Builds

Every character equips a **primary and a secondary profession**, drawing on the ability pools and stat growth of both. Mix them, exploit the synergies, and build units that would be illegal in a more polite game. There are eighteen professions across the three factions — soldiers and shieldbearers, archers and musketmen, pyromancers and cryomancers, assassins, berserkers, paladins, and more.

Every level-up offers perks from tiered pools — Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary — and each character can only carry so many, so upgrades shape the role they grow into. Combine Glass Cannon with Sacrificial Pact and a Regenerating aura, and you've built a one-character army that gets stronger the closer it gets to death.

> Ashes & Blood is a sandbox of strategy, designed to be broken by those clever enough to do it.

## About Small Strategy

Small Strategy is an independent game studio building deep, systems-driven strategy games for players who love to tinker, optimize, and break the rules.

Ashes & Blood is our debut title — an ambitious fusion of 4X world-building and grid-based tactical combat, built in Unity for PC (Windows, macOS, and Linux) with a Steam release ahead. It's the kind of game we always wanted to play: hard-hitting, nostalgic, dark low-fantasy, and generous with the freedom to be clever.
